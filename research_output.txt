Query: What is a ribosome?
Topic: Ribosome
Summary: A ribosome is a ribonucleoprotein particle made of ribosomal RNA (rRNA) and proteins, consisting of two subunits (small and large). It is the cellular machinery that translates messenger RNA (mRNA) into polypeptide chains, thereby synthesizing proteins. Ribosomes are found in the cytoplasm of all living cells—free-floating or attached to the endoplasmic reticulum in eukaryotes—and are essential for gene expression.
